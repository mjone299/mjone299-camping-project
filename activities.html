Things to Do While Camping

Camping in Idaho is about more than finding a place to sleep. There are so many ways to enjoy the outdoors and make memories with family and friends.

Whether you enjoy relaxing around the campsite or exploring the outdoors, Idaho has something for everyone.

Hiking

Idaho has trails ranging from easy walks to challenging mountain hikes. Hiking is a great way to explore forests, lakes, rivers, and mountain scenery.

Fishing

Fishing is a popular camping activity in Idaho. Many lakes, rivers, and streams provide opportunities to spend a relaxing day outdoors.

Boating and Water Activities

During the warmer months, Idaho's lakes and rivers are great places for boating, swimming, paddleboarding, and kayaking.

Relaxing at Camp

Sometimes the best part of camping is simply slowing down. Enjoy a campfire, play games, cook a meal, read a book, or spend time with family and friends.

Wildlife in Idaho

Seeing wildlife can be one of the most memorable parts of camping. Deer are common in many Idaho forests and outdoor areas, so keep your distance and enjoy watching them in their natural habitat.
