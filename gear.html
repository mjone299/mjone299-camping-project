Camping Gear Checklist

Having the right gear can make a camping trip much more comfortable. Use this checklist when getting ready for your next Idaho adventure.

Shelter and Sleeping

  • Tent or camping trailer
  • Sleeping bags
  • Sleeping pads or air mattresses
  • Pillows and blankets
  • Camping chairs

Cooking and Food

  • Camp stove or cooking equipment
  • Fuel
  • Cooler
  • Food and snacks
  • Drinking water
  • Cooking utensils
  • Plates, cups, and silverware
  • Trash bags

Outdoor Essentials

  • Flashlights or headlamps
  • Extra batteries
  • First-aid kit
  • Map or navigation device
  • Weather-appropriate clothing
  • Rain gear
  • Sunscreen
  • Bug spray

Don't Forget

Before leaving, double-check your campsite reservation, campground rules, weather conditions, and any current fire restrictions. A final checklist can help make sure nothing important gets left behind.
