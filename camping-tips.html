Camping Tips

A little preparation can make a camping trip much easier and more enjoyable. Before leaving home, take some time to plan your destination, check the weather, and make sure you have everything you need.

Plan Ahead

Choose your campground before you leave and check whether reservations are required. Look at the weather forecast and learn about any current fire restrictions or campground rules.

Pack Smart

Make a checklist before packing. Remember important items such as food, water, clothing, sleeping equipment, cooking supplies, flashlights, and first-aid supplies.

Stay Safe

Keep food and scented items stored properly, stay aware of your surroundings, and follow posted campground and trail rules. Always let someone know where you are going when heading into the outdoors.

Leave No Trace

Help keep Idaho's outdoors beautiful by cleaning up your campsite, disposing of waste properly, and respecting plants, wildlife, and other campers.
