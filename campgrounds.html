Idaho Campgrounds

Idaho has many beautiful places to camp, from mountain forests to lakes and rivers. Here are a few places to consider when planning your next camping adventure.

Before heading out, always check current campground rules, availability, weather conditions, and fire restrictions.

Before You Go

  • Check the weather forecast.
  • Make sure you have enough food and water.
  • Check campground availability and rules.
  • Follow Idaho fire restrictions.
  • Leave your campsite cleaner than you found it.
